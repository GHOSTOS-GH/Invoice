    64→### Calculations (mirror stats_screen.dart)
    65→- `filterByPeriod`: week = last 7 days (inclusive of today), month = last 30 days, all = everything. Comparison on `startOfDay(createdAt)`.
    66→- `totalCA` = sum of `invoiceTotal(inv.items)` (subtotal, before discount/tax — matches Flutter `invoiceTotal`).
    67→- `panierMoyen` = totalCA / factureCount (0 if none).
    68→- `plusGrosse` = max invoiceTotal.
    69→- `articlesVendus` = sum of `invoiceTotalQuantity`.
    70→- `buildCaSeries` — daily buckets (7 or 30) keyed by date; monthly buckets keyed by `YYYY-MM`, sorted chronologically from earliest invoice month.
    71→- `buildStatusRepartition` — sum of invoiceTotal grouped by `InvoiceStatus`, filtered to > 0.
    72→- `buildTopClients` — sum of invoiceTotal grouped by `clientName`, sorted desc, top 5.
    73→- `buildTopProducts` — sum of `item.quantity` grouped by `item.name`, sorted desc, top 5.

   197→### CSV Import screen — full pipeline
   198→- `ScreenHeader` with `UploadCloud` icon and a back button → `navigate("invoices")`.
   199→- **Drop zone**: large dashed border, dragover turns blue (`border-[#2563EB] bg-[#2563EB]/5`), click-to-browse fallback. Validates `.csv` extension + MIME type.
